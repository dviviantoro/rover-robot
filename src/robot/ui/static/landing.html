A voice you can talk to,
with nothing in the cloud

It listens, understands and answers out loud in Bahasa Indonesia, English and Arabic. Speech recognition, the language model and the voice all run on a single Jetson Orin Nano on the robot itself — no account, no API key, and no sentence ever leaves the room.

It hears you in a busy room

Silero voice detection finds where speech starts and stops, and three measured gates throw away what was never speech — so the robot does not answer the air conditioning.

It answers before it finishes thinking

A short acknowledgement goes out the moment your sentence lands, then the reply streams clause by clause as the model writes it — rather than a long silence and a paragraph.

It knows your documents

Drop a PDF in and it is retrieved at the moment it is relevant, answered in the language the question was asked in — not the language the document happened to be written in.

It moves while it speaks

Every reply carries what the body should do with it. An STM32 owns the motors and stops them on its own if the Jetson goes quiet, so a crash cannot leave the robot driving.
